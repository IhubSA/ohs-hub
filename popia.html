POPIA and data protection

How OHS Hub is designed to help you handle employee and incident information responsibly under the Protection of Personal Information Act.

Draft for legal review. This page must be reviewed by a qualified legal adviser before OHS Hub launches. Highlighted items in [brackets] still need to be completed.

Built-in safeguards

  • Company separation: every record belongs to one company, and the database refuses access from any other company.
  • Role-based access: employees see approved documents and their own reports and actions; managers see what their role requires; auditors have read-only access.
  • Confidential incidents: can be restricted to named, authorised people.
  • Data minimisation: workforce records don't ask for ID numbers or medical history. Injury details are captured only where an incident requires them.
  • Private files: evidence and certificates are stored privately and opened through short-lived links.
  • Audit trail: significant changes are logged with who made them and when.
  • AI requests: only the information needed for the requested draft is sent, and your records aren't used to train AI models.

Your responsibilities as the responsible party

Your company decides what OHS information to record, who may access it and how long to keep it. OHS Hub gives you the tools; your Information Officer remains accountable for your processing.
