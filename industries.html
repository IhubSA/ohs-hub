Industries we serve

OHS Hub's core modules work across sectors. Here is what each industry can use today, and what is still on the way.

Construction

Available now

  • Task-specific risk assessments and method statements
  • Site inspection checklists for scaffolding, excavations and housekeeping
  • Contractor registration and site access (later release)
  • Incident reporting from site, on a phone

Mining

Core modules now; mining modules planned

  • All core OHS modules: risk, inspections, incidents, actions and documents
  • Legal register structure ready for Mine Health and Safety Act 29 of 1996 requirements
  • Mining-specific modules and reporting are on the roadmap, not yet available

Manufacturing

Available now

  • Machine and workshop inspections
  • Equipment SOPs and lock-out procedures drafted with AI
  • PPE issue records (later release)
  • Incident trends by site and department

Engineering and maintenance

Available now

  • Job safety analyses and job hazard analyses
  • Equipment register with inspection schedules (later release)
  • Corrective actions linked to defects

Logistics and warehousing

Available now

  • Racking, forklift and housekeeping checklists
  • Near-miss reporting from any employee
  • Recurring inspection schedules per site

Transport

Available now

  • Vehicle inspection checklists
  • Driver licence and competency expiry tracking (later release)
  • Incident investigation for road events

Facilities management

Available now

  • Multi-site schedules and dashboards
  • Fire safety and emergency plan documents
  • Contractor management (later release)

General commercial businesses

Available now

  • A baseline risk assessment and emergency plan to start from
  • Simple incident reporting for all staff
  • Clear dashboards without enterprise complexity

OHS consultants

Available now

  • Separate companies for each client, with isolated data
  • One login across the companies you're invited to
  • Consistent templates you can reuse per client
