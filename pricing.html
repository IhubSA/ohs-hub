Pricing

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial of Professional. Commercial pricing is being finalised, so contact us for a quote for your team size.

Pricing questions

Do I need a card to start the trial?

No. You can start a 14-day trial without payment details. Online payment isn't switched on yet, so we'll contact you about billing before your trial ends.

Are prices in rand and do they include VAT?

All prices are quoted in South African rand (ZAR). Quotes state clearly whether VAT is included.

What happens if we need more users or sites?

You can move up a plan at any time. Enterprise customers get limits set to their needs.

What counts as an AI generation?

Each draft or section regeneration requested from the AI counts as one generation. Your monthly allowance depends on your plan, and you can see your usage in your account.
