Pricing questions

Do I need a card to start?

No. The Free plan has no time limit, and paid plans start with a 14-day trial without payment details. Online payment isn't switched on yet, so we'll contact you about billing before your trial ends.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you haven't chosen a paid plan, your organisation moves to the Free plan. Your records are kept, and modules that aren't in the Free plan become read-only until you upgrade.

Are prices in rand and do they include VAT?

All prices are in South African rand (ZAR). The note under the plans says whether VAT is included.

What happens if we need more users or sites?

Paid plans can add extra users for the monthly amount shown on each plan, or you can move up a plan at any time. For more than 50 users, talk to us about custom limits.

Can we pay annually?

Yes. Annual billing costs the same as 10 months, so you get two months free.

What counts as an AI generation?

Each draft or section regeneration requested from the AI counts as one generation. Your monthly allowance depends on your plan, and you can see your usage in your account.

Not sure which plan fits?

Start free, or tell us how many people, sites and contractors you have and we'll recommend one.
