First drafts in minutes. Final say stays with your competent person.

Tell OHS Hub about the task, site, equipment and people involved. It drafts a structured document using your company details and approved templates, then flags what is missing.

  • Standard operating procedures
  • Safe work procedures
  • Risk assessments
  • Job safety analyses
  • Inspection checklists
  • Toolbox talks
  • Emergency response plans
  • Incident investigation reports

Every draft can be edited, reviewed, approved and kept under document control, with version numbers, effective dates and review reminders.

AI-generated content is a starting point. It must be checked and approved by a competent person before use. OHS Hub never marks a document as compliant because AI wrote it, and any legal reference it can't match to a verified source is labelled "needs verification".
