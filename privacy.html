Privacy policy

How iHubSA collects, uses and protects personal information on the OHS Hub website and platform.

1. Who we are

OHS Hub is operated by iHubSA [REGISTERED COMPANY NAME AND REGISTRATION NUMBER], Fish Hoek, Western Cape, South Africa ("we", "us"). For POPIA purposes our Information Officer is Brent Proctor, contactable at enquiries@ihub-sa.co.za.

2. Two roles we play

Website visitors and account holders: we are the responsible party for the information you give us when you contact us, register or manage your subscription.

Customer OHS records: when your company records employees, incidents, training or other OHS information in the platform, your company is the responsible party and we process that information on its behalf as an operator, under our terms.

3. Information we collect

  • Contact enquiries: name, email address, company, industry, company size and your message.
  • Accounts: name, email address, job title, company details, and sign-in and security logs.
  • Platform records entered by customers: workforce details, risk, inspection, incident, training and document records, including photographs and evidence files.
  • Technical information: IP address (stored only as a one-way hash for abuse prevention), browser type and error logs.

We don't use advertising cookies or third-party tracking on this website.

4. Why we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide the service you sign up for.
  • To send service notifications such as approvals, reminders and overdue actions.
  • To keep the platform secure, prevent abuse and keep audit trails.
  • To meet legal obligations.

5. Special personal information

Incident and health-related records may include special personal information. Access is limited to roles your company authorises, confidential incidents are restricted further, and these records are never used for any purpose other than providing the service.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to its processing, by emailing enquiries@ihub-sa.co.za. If your information is held as part of your employer's OHS records, we'll pass your request to your employer. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is controlled by database-level security rules for each company, files are private and shared only through short-lived links, and significant actions are logged.
