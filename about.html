About OHS Hub

OHS Hub is built by iHubSA, a South African company that turns paperwork, spreadsheets and manual processes into secure online systems.

Why we built it

Safety professionals spend too much of their time chasing paper: risk assessments in one folder, inspection sheets in another, incident reports in an inbox and corrective actions in a spreadsheet nobody updates. OHS Hub brings those records into one connected system, so the time goes back to the work that protects people.

What we believe

  • Competent people make the decisions. AI drafts and suggests. Your safety professionals review and approve.
  • Numbers must be real. Dashboards and reports come only from records in your system.
  • Legal references must be verifiable. Anything not matched to a verified source is labelled as needing verification.
  • Your data is yours. Each company's records are isolated at database level and handled in line with POPIA.

Who we are

iHubSA is based in Fish Hoek, Western Cape, and builds business systems, databases, dashboards and SaaS platforms for South African organisations.
