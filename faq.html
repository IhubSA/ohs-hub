Getting started

What is OHS Hub?

A web application that brings risk assessments, inspections, incidents, corrective actions, safety documents and compliance tracking into one system, with AI to help draft safety documentation.

Do I need to install anything?

No. OHS Hub runs in a web browser on computers, tablets and phones.

How long is the free trial?

14 days on the Professional plan. No card details are needed to start.

AI and compliance

Will AI-generated documents make us compliant?

No software can guarantee compliance. AI drafts are a starting point that a competent person must review, adjust and approve. OHS Hub records who reviewed and approved each document.

Does the AI decide our risk ratings?

No. Risk scores are calculated by the system from your approved matrix. The AI can suggest hazards and controls, which you can accept, change or reject.

Where do the legal references come from?

From the legal register, where requirements are added and verified through a review process. If a reference can't be matched to a verified source, it is clearly marked as needing verification.

Is our data used to train AI models?

Your records are sent to the AI provider only when you ask for a draft, and only the information that draft needs. We use provider settings that don't allow your data to be used for model training. Details are in the privacy policy.

Security and data

Can other companies see our records?

No. Every record belongs to one company, and access is enforced by the database itself, not just hidden in the screens. This separation is tested automatically.

Where is our data stored?

In a managed PostgreSQL database and private file storage provided by Supabase. Files are only available through short-lived, signed links.

Who owns the data?

You do. You can export your records, and you can ask for them to be deleted when you leave.

How do you handle POPIA?

We collect only what each workflow needs, restrict sensitive records such as injury details and confidential incidents to authorised roles, and keep an audit trail. See our POPIA page for details.

Account and support

Can one person work across several companies?

Yes. A consultant can be invited to several client companies and switch between them, with each company's data kept separate.

How do I get help?

Email enquiries@ihub-sa.co.za or use the contact form on this site.
