AI SOP Generator

Guided SOP builder with an AI first draft

Fill in a structured form. OHS Hub drafts each section, checks that every required section is present, and highlights anything it couldn't complete.

  • Title and reference number
  • Purpose and scope
  • Site and department
  • Responsible persons
  • Required competencies and authorisations
  • Required PPE
  • Equipment and materials
  • Prerequisites
  • Identified hazards and precautions
  • Step-by-step work instructions
  • Critical control measures
  • Emergency response procedures
  • Required records
  • Author, reviewer and approver
  • Issue and review dates

Regenerate a single section without overwriting the rest. Every generated version and every edit is kept. Approved SOPs can be printed or exported.

AI Risk Assessment Generator

Risk assessments scored by your methodology

Supports baseline, issue-based and task-specific risk assessments, job safety analyses and job hazard analyses, using configurable templates.

  • Activity, task and hazard
  • Persons exposed
  • Potential consequences
  • Existing controls
  • Initial likelihood, severity, score and rating
  • Additional controls, following the hierarchy of controls
  • Residual likelihood, severity, score and rating
  • Responsible person and completion date
  • Approval and review details

Choose a 3×3 or 5×5 matrix and set your own labels and rating thresholds. Scores are calculated by the database from your approved formula. The AI can suggest hazards and controls but can't change a score.

Digital Inspections and Audits

Inspections from template to close-out

Generate checklist templates for general workplaces, construction sites, workshops, vehicles, machinery, electrical installations, fire safety, housekeeping, PPE and warehouses. Edit, group, reorder and approve every item before it's used.

  • Scheduled, recurring and ad hoc inspections, assigned to an inspector
  • Responses: compliant, non-compliant, not applicable, not inspected
  • Comments, photo evidence and risk classification on findings
  • Any non-compliant item can raise a corrective action
  • Inspection reports, follow-up inspections and close-out verification
  • Mobile-friendly capture that keeps your answers if the signal drops and sends them when it returns

Incident Reporting and Investigation

A complete incident lifecycle

Anyone in your organisation can report injuries, near misses, dangerous occurrences, property damage, occupational illnesses, environmental events and unsafe conditions, with photos and witnesses.

  • Investigator assignment, milestones and an evidence register
  • Witness statements and an event timeline
  • Immediate causes, underlying causes and contributing factors
  • 5 Whys and cause-and-effect analysis
  • Links to existing risk assessments and SOPs
  • Reviewer comments, final approval and close-out
  • Confidential reporting, visible only to people you authorise

AI can help structure evidence and suggest questions to ask. Its suggestions are labelled as unverified and are never recorded as an established cause.

Statutory reporting: configurable, versioned rules help you assess whether an event may need external notification, and track the responsible person, due date, submission and evidence. Rules must be verified by your organisation before they are relied on.

South African Legal Compliance Register

Business and Enterprise plans

A legal register you can trust

Start with the Occupational Health and Safety Act 85 of 1993, and add your own company requirements. Industry frameworks such as the Mine Health and Safety Act 29 of 1996 can be added for mining customers.

  • Legal reference, requirement and applicability
  • Applicable site or activity, and the responsible person
  • Compliance evaluation with supporting evidence and findings
  • Corrective actions linked to each gap
  • Assessment date, review date, source reference and version
  • A clear distinction between legislation, recommended practice and company requirements

A requirement only shows as verified once a second competent person has checked it against the official source and recorded how. If anyone changes the wording, it goes back to "needs verification". OHS Hub helps you manage compliance; it doesn't guarantee it.

Document Control

Controlled documents

  • A document library with categories and reference numbers
  • Draft, in review, approved, obsolete and archived statuses
  • Review and approval workflows, with independent approval enforced when your policy requires it
  • Version numbers, revision history, change summaries and effective dates
  • Review-date reminders and employee acknowledgements
  • Access permissions by role

When a new version is approved, the previous one is kept with its full audit history. Drafts are always clearly marked so nobody works from an unapproved procedure.

Corrective Action Management

One corrective action register

Actions arrive from risk assessments, inspections, audits, incident investigations, legal compliance assessments and management reviews.

  • Unique reference and a link back to the source record
  • Priority, responsible person and due date
  • Evidence attachments
  • Verification requirements and outcomes
  • Reminders before the due date and when overdue

An action that needs verification can't be closed until someone other than the responsible person has confirmed it worked.

Training and Competency Tracking

Business and Enterprise plans

Training and competency

Safety Reporting and Analytics

Reports from real records

  • Monthly and quarterly OHS reports
